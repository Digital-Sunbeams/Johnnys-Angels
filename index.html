Why people call

Nine hours is a long time for a dog to be alone.

Most of our families work full days or travel. They don't want to uproot their pets, drive across town, or hand a key to a stranger from an app. They want someone they trust to come to their home, keep the routine exactly as it is, and text a photo when the tail starts wagging.

That's the whole idea here. Your pets stay where they're most comfortable, and I come to them. I'm Natasha: one person, the same person, on every single visit.

Tailored care

Every dog gets their own plan.

Before the first visit, I ask a lot of questions, what your dog eats and when, where the leash lives, which route you usually walk, what scares them, what makes them light up, what you'd normally do at four in the afternoon. Then I run every visit that way.

Their routine, kept

Meals at your times, medication on your schedule, the same route they always walk. Your dog keeps the day they already know and love, just with a new friend holding the leash.

Their pace

Some dogs want the whole hour on a lap. Some want the longest walk of their week. Seniors get extra gentleness, puppies get extra patience, and every visit is shaped around what your dog loves most.

Their quirks, accommodated

Won't eat unless someone sits nearby. Hides when it thunders. Only drinks from a specific bowl. Explain what your dog needs, and it will be written down and followed.

About

One woman, a few dogs, and attention for everyone.

Hi, I'm Natasha. I've been caring for dogs for many years, and I still get a little excited on the drive to every visit. My favorite part of this work is getting to know each dog, because every one of them is different, and learning their particular personality never gets old.

I'm not a company. It's just me. And because it's just me, I can do the things a company can't: stay a few extra minutes when a dog is feeling nervous, send you an extra photo just because they looked too cute not to, and remember exactly how your dog likes things done.

When I come to your home, it's just me and your pets. I talk to them, play with them, tell them they're a good girl or a good boy, and if the zoomies break out, I could watch forever. I don't bring my own treats or toys: I'll ask you to leave out whatever your dog loves and is allowed, because your rules are the rules.

The truth is, a dog is happiest with you. My job is to be the next best thing until you're home: belly rubs, walks, good company, with particular attention to what makes your dog happy. More than anything, I want you to know this: whatever helps your dog feel at ease, that's what I'll do. Dogs of every size are welcome, and so is the rest of the household: cats, birds, and whoever else needs care. My goal is that coming home is the best part of your day, with a happy, content companion waiting at the door.

Questions

Asked and answered

What does a visit include?

A full hour: feeding, fresh water, bathroom, a real walk, and play, plus a photo or video update before I leave. If your pets need more than an hour, just say so and the visit is shaped to fit.

How big can my dog be?

Any size. Small dogs, big dogs, and everything between are welcome. Book a meet-and-greet and I'll get to know your dog first.

What animals will you watch?

Mostly dogs, and I've cared for cats, birds, and just about everything with a food bowl. Short of an alligator, bring it on.

What areas do you cover?

The whole Las Vegas valley: Summerlin, Henderson, North Las Vegas, and out to Boulder City. The first ten minutes of travel are included; farther out adds a small travel charge, quoted up front.

How does vacation care work?

Two visits a day while you're away, or I stay overnight at your place. Mail and plants are handled happily, more housesitting requests we can easily figure out, and photos and videos arrive every day, so you can enjoy the trip.

Will you stay overnight?

Yes. Overnights in your home start at $40 a night and include the evening and morning routines. Midday visits can be added for long days.

Can my dog stay at your place instead?

Yes. Staying overnight at my cozy Summerlin home starts at $40 a night, depending on availability. We'll sort the details at the meet-and-greet, and most dogs settle in fast.

Can you give medication?

Yes, at no extra charge, pills, liquids, insulin, eye drops, and the ones that need to be hidden in cheese. Just show me how to administer and the timing.

How is this different from a pet-sitting app?

With an app, your sitter can change trip to trip, and you often don't meet them until they're at your door. With me it's the same person every time: we meet before you ever book, and I get to know your dog by name, routine, and personality.

What if it isn't a good fit?

You'll be told, kindly and quickly. We'll figure it out together, and the goal is always a happy dog. Either way, you'll get an honest, caring answer.

What do dogs think of all this?

No complaints yet, and the tail wagging suggests a strong approval rating.
